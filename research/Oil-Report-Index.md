# Oil Crisis — Research Index

*A running research project tracking how the world's oil buffers — the Strategic Petroleum Reserve, commercial inventories in the US, Europe, and Asia — are depleting under the dual chokepoint crisis (Strait of Hormuz + Red Sea) and the Russian refinery front. Started as a stress test on Jun 30, 2026; updated with every new official data print.*

*Everything public lives on the site — **the Depletion Ledger** — at [oil-report.me-fce.workers.dev](https://oil-report.me-fce.workers.dev/). (Temporary URL; a permanent domain is coming.)*

## The pages

### [The dashboard](https://oil-report.me-fce.workers.dev/)
The interactive view: Brent since January, gasoline and diesel, the SPR drawdown with its floor lines, the likely outcomes, and the flip from surplus to drawdown. Charts, not prose.

### [The model, in plain language](https://oil-report.me-fce.workers.dev/model/)
What the model is, where every number comes from, the three possible futures with their odds, why the odds only move on pre-decided events, and what the model can't do.
