- Tehran talks: Saudi Defense Minister Khalid bin Salman met President Pezeshkian and Security Council secretary Rezaei on October 26, with Pakistan's army chief and Turkey's intelligence chief participating (CNBC). Participants agreed to continue security talks, according to Iranian state media cited by Bloomberg; no US–Iran agreement has been reported. Two Iranian security officials told Reuters on October 23 that Iran has plans to target Gulf undersea communications cables if attacked again.
- Egypt's parliament authorized military support for Saudi Arabia in a Monday vote (AP/Reuters): el-Sisi is authorized to "undertake necessary" measures should Riyadh request assistance. Sisi rules out strikes on Yemen.
- CNN reports US forces could begin major operations within 96 hours of a presidential order. Officials expect an order most likely after the November 3 midterms, but Trump has not made a decision. This is readiness after an order, not a countdown.
- UKMTO's October 26 warning describes an October 23 projectile strike that disabled an outbound tanker. The crew were safe; no damage assessment was available. The separate IRGC two-supertanker mine claim remains unverified. Neither establishes a confirmed vessel loss.
- On October 23, DOE authorized Phillips 66 to borrow up to 400,000 barrels of SPR crude. Deliveries reduce stocks; repayment returns the borrowed oil plus a premium. The authorized amount is not a confirmed delivery.
- EIA stocks, week ending October 16: SPR 282.7M barrels; diesel and heating oil 104.3M, 9.7% below last year.
