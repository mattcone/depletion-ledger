## SPR release mechanics — the exchange program (verified), explains the taper

Research note (working conclusions, facts/interpretations separated) reviewed and

volume is a ceiling) → companies bid (ranked on additional premium, then
volume; financial guarantees) → awards → contractor delivery schedules (DOE
approval; title passes at custody transfer; physical delivery — not the
award — moves inventory) → future return obligation.
