## What this means for the model

1. **§7A becomes a §9B front.** Jazan is product capacity (gasoline + ULSD — exactly the scarce assets) at the end of the bypass the Houthis are blocking. It is the second Saudi processing loss after Abqaiq (Jul 27) — adding a repair clock, not just a flow disruption.
2. **Dual-chokepoint coordination is now stated, not inferred.** Houthi attacks are targeting "the principal Saudi alternative route to the blockage at Hormuz" — the two chokepoints are being fought on the same calendar; the model's key offsetting assumption (a strait failure is absorbed by the Red Sea route) is gone.
3. **The US blockade is working, quantified.** Floating crude 29M (from ~90M) is the first hard number: ~⅔ of pre-war floating stock is gone.
4. **ANZ is the base case, in analyst-house words.** The institutional read has caught up with the model.
5. **Price confirms the trajectory, not a new regime.** Brent $98.62 toward $100 extends the Sep 7 $97.89 six-week high; the **Sep 9 STEO revision** (the first after the Sep 6–7 escalation) is the next hard signal.
