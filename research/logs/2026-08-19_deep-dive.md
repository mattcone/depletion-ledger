## C. Russia & global product shortages — rationing shows up in the data

- **Kirishi (KINEF) — Russia's #2 refinery, only NW-Russia plant, ~400 kb/d — hit Aug 30, "completely shut"** (UA.NEWS, Sep 2). Plus Novatek's Ust-Luga refinery + export terminal (hit Sep 1; terminal already hit twice in early August). Capacity-down estimates: Ukrainian General Staff **42.74%**, IEA ">20%", Russian Forbes **54%** damaged.
- **Domestic crisis made visible:** gasoline available at only **28% of stations** nationwide (Aug 19, vs 41% the prior week); **90% of Moscow stations out of AI-92**; per-customer purchase caps (40–60 L), QR-code rationing, odd/even license plates in nearly every region; National Guard deployed to stations; record cash withdrawals in rubles; FAS opened **41 price-abuse cases** (Aug 19–20); **Euro-2/3/4 (low-grade) gasoline approved for ~1 year** — SPIMEX trading began Aug 18.
- **Belarus:** rail fuel imports into Russia **+25x YoY** Jan–Jul; record July deliveries (212K t petrol + 162K t diesel); Novopolotsk under maintenance in September.
- **Central Asia spillover (new):** petrol prices up **Tajikistan +12–13%, Kyrgyzstan +10%, Uzbekistan +11–11.5%**; Kyrgyzstan (>90% of petrol imported from Russia) has ~6 weeks of cover and is asking Azerbaijan/Kazakhstan for help.
- **Bangladesh:** gas supply to industry <50% of demand; CNG load-shedding peaked at 3,595 MW (~Aug 30); Ashuganj fertilizer plant dark for over a year.
- **Airlines:** China's Big Three (Air China, China Eastern, China Southern) combined **$1.21B loss** on jet fuel; domestic fuel surcharge added Aug 2026 (RMB 40/70).
- **Retail:** AAA US national avg **$4.1203** (Sep 2); Australia 91-unleaded **205.1 c/L** (+4.9 WoW), diesel 252.2; Russia petrol avg **70 ₽/L** (+7.4% H1), diesel +12%, some regions >100 ₽/L.
