   drone attack hit the field in Chamchamal district, Sulaymaniyah governorate, and
   suspended production. The regional electricity ministry said the disruption took about
   2,800 MW of generation offline; no group had claimed responsibility as of the pass
   (Al Jazeera; JPost; Anadolu; Shafaq; Rudaw).
3. **Iraq–US agreement on a Hormuz transit lane near Oman (Nov 8, Reuters).** Iraqi FM
   Fuad Hussein said Iraq reached understandings with the US to export oil through Hormuz
   via a lane near Oman (AJ interview). If implemented, a state-sanctioned route —
   watch the next Kpler print for whether transits respond.

   also said the US has identified 38 Iranian bridges that could be destroyed within an
   hour if fighting resumes (the count is unverified).
5. **Explosions on Qeshm Island and in Bandar Abbas (Nov 8, UNCONFIRMED).** Tasnim

   from the Nov 7 southern-Iran reports (carried from the Nov 8 log).

   FM's spokesman — the first reported visit since the war began.

   report that Iran had dropped its demands for control of the strait and transit fees;

   Iran's demands "outrageous and ridiculous." Araghchi: no mediator travel since the US
   midterms; talks entering a fifth week. Qatar's PM is to meet Trump and Vance (AJ,
   Nov 9).

   Nov 9).** Russia's second-largest refinery, Leningrad Oblast (~404 kb/d). Governor
   Drozdenko confirmed the drone strike; local reports of three processing units hit; a
   woman killed by debris elsewhere in the region. Kirishi was already listed as halted
