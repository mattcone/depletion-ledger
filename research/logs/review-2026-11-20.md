     through a ship-to-ship transfer in the Gulf was spotted — possibly Iran's
     first crude shipment in four months (Reuters, via TankerTrackers)".
   - **Evidence:** TankerTrackers told Reuters the loading was under way "on
     Thursday", Nov 19 (Iran International 202611192862, dated Nov 19 18:21
     GMT; Al Jazeera Nov 20). Madani's claim is conditional: *if* it clears

   - **Now:** "On Nov 19, TankerTrackers told Reuters a tanker was taking on
     Iranian crude from another ship in the Gulf; if it clears the blockade,
     it would be Iran's first seaborne crude export since mid-July." SUMMARY
     is fixed to match.

     Island to test US oil blockade". The quoted wording isn't in it. The

     satellite imagery of more than 14 tankers.

     paraphrase and flags that the article wasn't read.
