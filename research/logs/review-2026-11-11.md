  It touched mid-29s on Nov 5 and "rebounded to the high-USD 31s by the end of the week".

  mentions only "the results of the U.S. midterm elections", with no "Democratic sweep …

The JKM note is also internally inconsistent: "down $1.36 on the week", but 34.0 → 28.68 is
−5.32. The TTF series also switches basis from December futures (Oct 30) to "spot" (Nov 6),
so the −16.8% compares different contracts.
