## User-reported: gas tooltip text cut off (~14:00 UTC)

`p.note` as one line, and the new provenance notes run ~120 chars (the Sep 18/22 TTF
and Sep 18 JKM notes) — Chart.js tooltips don't wrap, so a single long line overflows
the canvas and clips. The Brent chart already solves this with a short `tip` field
(tooltip) vs long `note` (provenance); the gas data had only `note`. Fix: added
`tip?: string` to GasPt and set short tips on the five long points — TTF Sep 14
(€81.98 CFD × 1.1557 (converted)), Sep 18 (JOGMEC (pub. Sep 24), Oct delivery),
Sep 22 (€72.10 close quote × 1.1467 (converted)); JKM Sep 11 (assessed: mid-USD
28s (record Sep 10)), Sep 18 (JOGMEC (pub. Sep 24), Nov delivery) — and made
gasTooltip prefer `p.tip || p.note || default`. Full provenance notes kept in the
data untouched. Checked the other charts for the same problem: 10-yr (short source
label), diesel (longest note ~51 chars, fits), gasoline (value only), Brent/WTI
(tip already used), crack (value only), recession (short notes) — all fine. Staging
redeployed (new bundle); all five tips verified present in the live JS. Prod still
