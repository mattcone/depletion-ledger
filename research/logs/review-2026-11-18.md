1. **The Russia tally double-counted two plants.**
   - **Was:** Ilsky and Afipsky were added as new entries. The count went to
     "17 sites" even though both were already in the list (Ilsky Nov 15–16,
     Afipsky Nov 6).
   - **Why it's wrong:** the row is labeled "Refineries and industrial sites
     struck", and every other plant appears once. Earlier repeats, such as
     Ryazan and Novokuibyshevsk, are noted inside their own entries.
   - **Now:** the Nov 17–18 strikes are merged into the existing entries, and
     the dates read "Nov 15–16; Nov 17–18" and "Nov 6; Nov 17–18". The count
     is **15 sites**.
   - If Qwen meant to count strikes rather than sites, relabel the row instead
     and restore the entries.
2. **Ilsky/Afipsky attribution.**
   - **Was:** "drones struck the plant … per the regional operational
     headquarters (ASTRA)".
   - **Evidence:** the headquarters said only "fires broke out at the two
     facilities due to falling drone debris" and didn't name them. Local
     residents (via ASTRA) named Ilsky and Afipsky (UA.NEWS quote; Moscow
     Times).
   - **Afipsky capacity:** 6.25 Mt/yr conflicts with Moscow Times (9.1 Mt), so
     it was dropped.
3. **EIA release time.**
   - **Was:** "noon ET" in the Nov 18 watch item and in the cascade.
   - **Evidence:** EIA's schedule lists no exception for this week, so the
     standard 10:30 a.m. ET applies. Noon was the Veterans Day schedule for
     Nov 12. The log itself says 10:30.
4. **"Kpler State of the Strait" (Houthi item).** The newsletter isn't
   Kpler's; it cites Kpler (see the Nov 17 review). It now reads "via the
   'State of the Strait' newsletter".
5. **#211 = ADNOC was stated as fact.**
   - **Was:** "#211 — a time-late report of a Nov 17 strike on an ADNOC vessel
     13 NM north of Khasab … (UKMTO, BBC, Reuters)".
   - **Evidence:** UKMTO doesn't name the ship. ADNOC doesn't give a location
     beyond "the Strait of Hormuz". The Reuters and The National copies I read
     don't connect the two. The match fits: time-late, Nov 17, one missing.
     The BBC article cited in the log wasn't found.
   - **Now:** "It appears to be the ADNOC L&S ship …". SUMMARY now says "likely
     … unconfirmed". Restore the firm wording with a URL if the BBC makes the
     link.
6. **"Moscow has urged refineries to shift to lower-grade gasoline as quality
   output falls (Reuters via Moscow Times)".** It was Deputy PM Novak urging
   companies to produce more lower-grade gasoline. Fixed. The Rand halts moved
