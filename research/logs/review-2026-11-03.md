## Factual corrections

1. **Al Jusufi rescue service and “first crew death.”**
   - **Original:** dashboard called the rescuers “the Omani Navy” and described this as “the first crew member killed in a strait attack in this window.” The daily log called it the first crew death in a Hormuz attack and attributed rescue to the Omani Royal Navy.
   - **Replacement:** removed the “first” claim. The dashboard now says one crew member died and 13 were rescued; it notes that India's minister identified Oman's Coast Guard, while UKMTO's warning says the Royal Navy of Oman. Vessel status remains unknown per UKMTO.
   - **Sources:** [UKMTO API snapshot, Nov 3](../data/ukmto/ukmto-all-20261103T132313Z.json) (Warning 194-26 text); [Indian shipping minister's statement reproduced by NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/world-news/indian-sailor-killed-in-attack-on-cargo-vessel-al-jusufi-off-oman-coast-12274884); [AP, Nov 3](https://apnews.com/article/9ae5cec3d1f9cde02652f52f7f80fbce).
   - **Reason/uncertainty:** the official sources differ on the Omani service; both say 13 people were rescued. “First crew death” was too broad and could be read as a claim about the entire war. The exact vessel status remains unverified.
   - **Identity map:** changed the new record's `warningNumber` from null to `194-26`, which is printed in the archived UKMTO API body. Its temporary `canonical` ID remains `28082035` pending UKMTO re-slotting.

2. **Qeshm strike description.**
   - **Original:** “in response to a drone attack on a US aircraft”; the copy also said the IRGC intercepted eight US fighter jets and that the Pentagon said no US aircraft were in the area.
   - **Replacement:** CNN reports anonymous US officials described the strikes as defensive and said they followed Iranian fire at a US aircraft. The copy now attributes the account and says damage on Qeshm is not independently confirmed. The eight-jets claim and purported Pentagon denial were removed because I could not verify those specific statements in the available sources.
   - **Sources:** [CNN report via KESQ, Nov 3](https://kesq.com/news/national-politics/cnn-us-politics/2026/11/03/us-carried-out-limited-defensive-strikes-on-irans-qeshm-island-official-says/); [NBC News, Nov 2](https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/national-international/us-military-limited-strikes-iran-qeshm-island/3964813/); [AP, Nov 2](https://apnews.com/article/b4519ef86bafe2672e0855d7a48ce309) and [AP, Nov 3](https://apnews.com/article/183b3d32d9bf5241f744abb917e6713e).
   - **Reason/uncertainty:** reporting describes Iranian fire directed at a US aircraft; “drone attack on a US aircraft” could wrongly imply an unmanned US aircraft was attacked. The US description is anonymous, reports confirm explosions on Qeshm, and damage there is not independently verified. The removed Iranian and Pentagon claims remain unverified here, not disproven.

3. **Yaroslavl repeat-strike dates.**
   - **Original:** YANOS was said to have been struck Oct 27 and Nov 1, making the Nov 3 attack its third strike since Oct 27.
   - **Replacement:** removed those dates and the “third strike” claim. The entry now records the Nov 3 strike, governor-confirmed fire/infrastructure damage, two injured employees, and no confirmed production halt.
   - **Sources:** [Reuters, Nov 3](https://uk.marketscreener.com/news/ukrainian-drones-set-fire-to-major-russian-oil-refinery-local-governor-says--ce785fd9df88f521); [The Insider's Nov 3 chronology](https://theins.org/news/299074), which places the Oct 30, Oct 31, and Nov 1 attacks at Novokuibyshevsk, Ufa, and Kstovo respectively, and the Nov 2 strike at the Samara pipeline station.
