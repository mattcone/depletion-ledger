   - **Was:** "$80.17 (down $3.24 from the December contract's final
     settlement)". The wtiWeekly note and SUMMARY said the same.
   - **Evidence:** 83.98 − 80.17 = 3.81. The −$3.24 (−3.88%) is the January
     contract's change from its own Nov 20 settlement (about 83.41), per the
     wire.
   - **Now:** "January WTI at $80.17 (down $3.24)". The note explains that
     the gap to the plotted December final settlement is $3.81.
2. **Hodeidah toll.**
   - **Was:** "Houthi media now put that toll at four killed and 18 injured".
   - **Evidence:** 4/18 was the *initial* Nov 23 figure. Houthi media then
     said 10, and the Houthi telecoms department now says 17 workers killed
     and 11 wounded (Xinhua, Nov 24, "rises to 17"). The Sanaa health
     ministry separately says eight killed.
   - **Now:** "killed 17 workers … up from an initial four". It is attributed
     to Houthi officials, and AFP's inability to verify is noted.

   - **Was:** "drowning".
   - **Evidence:** most outlets, including Gulf News quoting his X post, have
     "underwater". "Drowning" appears in one outlet's paraphrase.
   - **Now:** "underwater".

   - **Was:** "Iran's state media on Nov 24 dismissed … (Mehr, via HNGN)".
   - **Evidence:** the denial was IRIB's, on Monday Nov 23 (The National,
