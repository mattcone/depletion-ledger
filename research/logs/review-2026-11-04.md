## Factual corrections

1. **Trump's “no immediate plans” statement.**
   - **Original:** “Trump said on Nov 4 ... (Bloomberg)” and the summary called it a “post-midterm” signal.
   - **Replacement:** Trump said this in a Financial Times interview on Nov 3, before polls closed. Reuters reported it on Nov 3. The New York Times and Wall Street Journal had separately reported that CENTCOM prepared a plan for Nov 4, with no presidential decision.
   - **Sources:** [Reuters, Nov 3, via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/us-has-no-immediate-plan-to-strike-iran-trump-tells-ft-4996958); [Reuters, Nov 4, via MarketScreener](https://uk.marketscreener.com/news/oil-rises-on-iran-war-uncertainty-after-trump-s-republicans-face-election-rout-ce785fdede80f324).
   - **Reason/uncertainty:** The interview was pre-election; calling it post-midterm reverses the sequence. “No immediate plans” is Trump's statement, not proof no strike plan existed. The separately reported contingency remains attributed to the NYT/WSJ.

2. **Candidate shadow output and staleness.**
   - **Original:** Nov 3 evening log recorded HORIZON 15.4/49.8/34.8 and STATE 0.8/99.2/0.0, attributing the difference to a daily staleness term. The summary repeated the staleness explanation.
   - **Replacement:** Corrected Nov 3 output to HORIZON 15.2/49.9/34.9 and STATE 0.5/99.5/0.0. Replayed with the logged inputs and `--today 2026-11-04`; the script reproduces the Nov 2/Nov 3 morning output exactly. Input age is reported metadata and does not enter the calculation.
   - **Evidence:** `research/model/branch_filter_candidate.py`; command and exact output reproduced during this review. The erroneous evening value is marked as a transcription error in the Nov 3 log.
   - **Uncertainty:** None about this replay; it verifies the script's output for those inputs, not the model's predictive quality.

3. **License 135 and tanker wording.**
   - **Original:** “three tankers ... under US license 135.”
   - **Replacement:** Windward tracked three tankers carrying Russian diesel as US-bound; one still appears headed for a US port. License 135 authorizes covered transactions and has not yet resulted in a US delivery; the ships themselves are not described as being under the license.
   - **Source:** [Windward, Nov 3](https://windward.ai/blog/only-one-russian-diesel-tanker-still-appears-bound-for-the-us/).
   - **Reason/uncertainty:** The original conflated a transaction authorization with vessel status. The destination remains a tracking assessment; no US delivery is confirmed.

4. **Election effects and market move.**
   - **Original:** Summary/log wording said Trump said both the Iran war and gasoline prices hurt Republicans, and linked the early oil move to the election and strike signal without a timestamp-specific qualification.
   - **Replacement:** Separates the claims: Reuters reported Trump acknowledged the Iran war played a role; AP voter-poll reporting cited high prices. The price note labels the early readings as an unsettled snapshot and says Reuters described volatile trading as investors weighed Washington's next move.
   - **Sources:** [Reuters, Nov 4, via MarketScreener](https://uk.marketscreener.com/news/oil-rises-on-iran-war-uncertainty-after-trump-s-republicans-face-election-rout-ce785fdede80f324); [AP voter poll](https://apnews.com/article/ba1689d8402415d26ad32f4fa80ab2f9); [Reuters early-trade report, via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/commodities-news/oil-edges-down-as-investors-await-us-midterm-election-results-4997127).
   - **Reason/uncertainty:** Reuters coverage records volatile price action at different times, so the pass's live quote should not be treated as a settled move or a single-cause response. The exact live quote provider was not retained in the repo; the timestamp is preserved, but that quote was not independently reconstructed here.

5. **Russian refinery-strike context.**

   - **Reason/uncertainty:** The prior wording blended a reported pause/announcement with the later resumption. The revised sentence reports sequence only; it does not infer why Ukraine resumed strikes.

6. **IRGC mine statement.**
