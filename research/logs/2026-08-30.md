## What changed (Aug 29 claim → Aug 30 reality)

- **Red Sea:** the Houthi embargo on Saudi shipping (Jul 20) has cut Saudi Bab el-Mandeb crude from **2.4M bpd (Jul) → 64K bpd (Aug), −97%**. Kpler: total Gulf exports **3.6M bpd in August — 82% below pre-war** (20M in Feb; May trough 1.4M). → **Both chokepoints are closed.** There is one valve (the Hormuz shadow corridor) and no working bypass.
- **Russia:** all major Lukoil refineries offline (Perm Aug 21; **NORSI — Russia's #4, 15M t/yr — Aug 26**). **21+ refinery strikes in August** (record, near-daily). Bans extended: producer diesel → **Sep 30**; non-producer gasoline/diesel → **Jan 31, 2027**; jet → **Nov 30, 2026**. Crimea under fuel emergency since June. → The ban calendar now spans all of Q4.
- **Asymmetry:** Atlantic-basin Saudi crude reroutes around the Cape or via SUMED (2.5M bpd cap; Sidi Kerir loadings ~1.3M): +10–15 days' voyage, record freight (VLCC $647K/day). To Asia, the Suez route roughly doubles the voyage → uneconomic: **the bypass is dead for East-of-Suez demand.** → Asia worst; US/Europe pay freight, not scarcity.

**Net:** the Oct/Nov question is no longer "does the corridor extend?" but "can *any combination* of the three fronts (Hormuz corridor, Red Sea, Russia) deliver Q4 product?"

## Aug 30 TL;DR

- Residual routes: **Fujairah (+1M), Kirkuk–Ceyhan (750K), SUMED (~1.3M loading / 2.5M cap), Cape routing.** Kpler's Gulf total 3.6M bpd (−82%) vs Goldman's 15–16M including the dark fleet — the gap is unverifiable, and that is itself the risk.
- Russia is the **third front**: simultaneous crude (Gulf) and product (Russia + Gulf refineries) scarcity — the regime that pins retail ~40% above pre-crisis while Brent unwinds.
- **Asia is now the most exposed region — China is the variable:** 2Q26 imports −32% QoQ, drawing *commercial* stockpiles (not the official SPR), product exports halted. Official reserve estimated **1.2–1.4B bbl** (all figures third-party estimates; China discloses nothing), untouched. Potential relief valve: China–Houthi safe-passage talks (Jul 28).
- **US Oct/Nov (§9A):** gasoline safe (net exporter). National diesel survivable in the base case (55–65M bbl, ~15–17 days, end-November). **PADD1 (East Coast) is the flashpoint in every branch** — 8–10 days cover by mid-November in the base case, single digits if the corridor lapses. Base-case retail diesel $5.30–5.80 by November.
- **Demand destruction:** IEA 2026 demand −1.6 mb/d (revised down ~510 kb/d in a month); self-limiting — December re-spike risk if the corridor holds and Brent falls.
- **Europe:** ARA trader floor 8.5–9M crossed ~late October (mid-October if the corridor lapses); GEF December 75–82% looks optimistic → revised 70–80%; visible European shortages expected **mid/late October**, not December.
