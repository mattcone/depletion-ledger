   re-cut. The public item now states the currently available source (21,

   remains the model's transit input; Windward is context only.
2. **Tracking caveat was blanket-wrong for Windward.** Kpler's counts exclude
   tracking-off vessels; Windward's EXPLICITLY INCLUDES them (9 of 21 dark).
   The watch item now describes each source's coverage separately and drops
   the blanket "exclude ships with tracking signals off" sentence.

   had 29 ships come out" — a single night's departures, not "an average of
   29 vessels a day" (the average wording is GlobalSecurity's summary, which

   overnight departure claim and drops "reconciles with neither independent
   count" — the periods (one night vs 24-hour totals), directions, and
   coverage (presidential vs tracker-defined) were never matched. A
   reporting-definition correction, not evidence about the underlying count.
