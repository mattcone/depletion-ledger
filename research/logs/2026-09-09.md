## 8. What this means for the model — REWEIGHT (regime event)

1. **This is a regime event under the update procedure** (tanker-war escalation + exclusion zone enforcement + first third-country base hit). Branch weights move (author's judgment):
   - **Corridor holds: ~15% → ~10%.** The shadow lanes are now inside a declared (if uncoordinated) exclusion zone and most traffic rides the Iranian-controlled route — "holds" now means "Iran-administered."
   - **Standoff drift: ~50–55% → ~50%.** Still the base case — the executed exchange-rate regime is *stable*, which is the definition of standoff. But it is a hotter standoff.
   - **Corridor lapses: ~30% → ~40%.** The exclusion zone + the tanker-at-berth threats (Kuwait/Bahrain) + 10 tankers gone in a week are each lapse accelerants. The US exchange rate makes de-escalation costlier, not easier.
2. **The Oman corridor is the swing factor.** If the IMO filing is real and Oman brokers a registered lane, the "corridor holds" branch reconstitutes *under Iranian management* — a structurally different (less free-flow, more revocable) corridor. Verify the filing before moving the weights back.
3. **§11 compression, round two:** the sequence is unchanged; most dates pull another ~1–3 weeks. The Sep 30 Russia ban (T-21) lands on a base that now includes Novorossiysk. The Polymarket "normal by Sep 30" settles ~0. Goldman's $120 scenario is now named by a major bank — the escalation tail has institutional cover.
4. **The EIA–market spread is a new input:** EIA 2H26 ~$90 vs spot >$100. If the spread widens past ~$10–15 into October, the market is pricing lapse >40% and the branch weights should move with it. If the Oct 7 STEO holds at ~$90 while spot stays >$100, EIA is wrong about "constraints, not closure" — that's the tell.
5. **Jazan's §9B entry is provisional:** "offline since at least late July (nameplate 400 kb/d); re-struck Sep 8; restart already slipped once (to late Aug)." The 2019 Abqaiq 7-day precedent no longer applies — this repair clock started weeks before the Sep 8 strike.
