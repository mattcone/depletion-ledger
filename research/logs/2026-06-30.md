## Original model structure (as carried forward)

- **SPR salt-cavern physics** — not a tank you can pump to empty. Floors: ~300M cavern-damage (engineering est., not a published DOE figure), ~250M GEF operational, ~180M hard-operable, ~70M DOE safe minimum.
- **"Products, not crude" thesis** — the IEA's 400M-barrel coordinated release + demand destruction held the crude price down; product inventories, not crude, are the scarce thing. When inventories hit operable floors and the corridor expires, the shock lands in the product price.
- **Europe winter cliff** — ARA gasoil depletion path to the physical trader floor (~8.5–9M bbl), with price-triggered political breaks expected before physical scarcity.
- **Asia** — primary importers (JP/KR/CN) buffered by stockpiles; secondary importers (SG/MY) and Australia are the exposed ones.
