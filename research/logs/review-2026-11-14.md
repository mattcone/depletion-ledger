1. **"Both benchmarks were on course for a second straight weekly decline."**
   Nov 13 was Friday's settlement, so the week had already closed. Now "Brent
   fell 2.5% for the week, its second straight weekly decline." I computed the
   2.5% from the series; HDFC's −2.4% was an intraday figure. WTI also fell 2.5%,
   but Rigzone gives no figure.
2. **"Mossad director Roman Gofman … (Rigzone)."** Rigzone doesn't name him; Axios
   and ToI do. The note now says "the Mossad director" with the Rigzone cite. The
   mine claims were also removed from the price narrative, because Rigzone didn't
   cite them as a driver.
3. **"The first US-confirmed Iranian launch at a Gulf host state since early
   September (ISW-CTP)."** What I found is Reuters calling it the first time
   since early September that *Iran has claimed* an attack on US assets in a Gulf
   country. The copy now uses that framing, attributed to Reuters.
4. **"Long queues formed at Volgograd petrol stations after the refinery halt
   (Archyde)."** No halt was confirmed: officials did not even confirm the
   refinery was hit. Now "lines formed at Volgograd gas stations after the Nov 13
   drone attack (Meduza)."
5. **"Core +4.9%" (PPI stats card and Fed paragraph).** 4.9% is the
   ex-food/energy/*trade* series. CNN's "core" (ex food and energy) is 5.0%.
   Both places now name the series.
