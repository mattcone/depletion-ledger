2. **Iranian vessel-fire claims.**

   - **Replacement:** The summary now says reports gave differing counts; the dashboard and judgment history refer to unverified vessel-fire claims without choosing one total. The daily log retains Al Jazeera's specific account as an attributed claim.
   - **Sources:** [Al Jazeera, Nov 5](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/5/iran-says-tankers-struck-in-hormuz-amid-fears-of-new-us-attack) relays the IRGC claim of five tankers and a tugboat; [AP, Nov 5](https://apnews.com/article/a35d13806844d99fda93de0a19de57fa) reports Iranian state TV's account of two tankers and a towboat; [Reuters, Nov 5](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/tankers-hit-hormuz-iran-keeps-up-pressure-after-us-vote-2026-11-05/) reports three more tankers said to have been damaged.

3. **Treasury sanctions date and scope.**

   - **Replacement:** Summary dates OFAC's designation to Nov 4, distinguishes the Nov 5 WSJ coverage, and names only jurisdictions listed in OFAC's announcement.

   - **Reason/uncertainty:** Treasury's notice names multiple jurisdictions but does not state the number 11. The count was removed rather than repeated without primary-source support. The governments themselves were not sanctioned in this action.

4. **TTF contract comparison.**

   - **Replacement:** The stats caption and summary identify Oct 30 as December delivery and Oct 23 as November delivery; the sentence no longer presents the change as a like-for-like move.
